United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

This project focuses on the practical application of quantitative project management (PERT/CPA) coupled with DevSecOps engineering principles to modernize public transit infrastructure and digital delivery systems.

Primary Goal

SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure

Target 9.1 & 9.4 Alignment

Target 9.1: Develop quality, reliable, sustainable and resilient infrastructure, including regional and transborder infrastructure, to support economic development and human well-being.

Engineering Contribution: Modern smart transportation systems (such as automated vehicle location, fleet telemetry, and real-time transit APIs) consist of interdependent cyber-physical deliverables. By replacing intuitive or static scheduling with PERT probabilistic modeling:

  • Hardware and cloud dependencies are rigorously sequenced, preventing bottlenecks during infrastructure modernization.
  • Critical paths identify high-risk integration stages early (e.g., GPS hardware testing, API ingestion gateways), preventing public transit deployment delays.
  • Automated DevSecOps pipelines ensure digital transport infrastructure is continuously audited for CVEs and configuration flaws before field rollout.
Supporting Goal

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Target 11.2 Alignment

Target 11.2: Provide access to safe, affordable, accessible and sustainable transport systems for all, notably by expanding public transport.

Engineering Contribution: Urban commuters heavily rely on predictable, high-frequency public transportation. The rollout of smart transit features—such as live bus tracking, digital ticketing, and passenger load estimation—directly determines commuter adoption of public transit over private fossil-fuel vehicles:

  • Mathematical variance calculation (σ2) gives urban transit authorities realistic confidence intervals for launch dates.
  • Z-score probability calculations allow transport directors to evaluate budget-deadline tradeoffs with data-driven accuracy rather than speculative estimations.
  • Enables municipalities to coordinate multiple parallel transit works without overlapping service disruptions.

Scope Boundaries and Objective Assessment

To maintain strict technical integrity, this software does not purport to directly eliminate urban emissions or manufacture transit physical hardware. Rather, it serves as a decision-support and pipeline automation tool:

  1. It provides reliable scheduling analytics under uncertainty for engineering teams deploying civic transport technology.
  2. It enforces DevSecOps quality gates so that public digital utilities deployed on cloud infrastructure (AWS) are safeguarded against cyber threats, data tampering, and service outages.
